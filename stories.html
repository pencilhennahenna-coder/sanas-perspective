Nek Dil Ali aur Ramzan ka Tohfa

By Sana Rahman

Ramzan ka mubarak mahina shuru ho chuka tha. Nanhe Ali ne is saal apna pehla roza rakha tha. Woh subah se bahut khush tha aur shaam ka besabri se intezar kar raha tha.

Ali ne khidki se bahar dekha. Pados mein rehne wali budhi Zohra Bi apni dehleez par khamosh baithi thi. Unka chehra bahut thaka hua aur udaas lag raha tha.

Ghar ke andar, Ali ki Ammi, Safia, kitchen mein Iftari ki taiyariyan kar rahi thi. Woh garam-garam pakode tal rahi thi aur tazza phal kaat rahi thi.

Ali dabe paon Zohra Bi ke ghar gaya. Usne dekha ke kitchen ka choolha thanda tha aur dastar-khwan par sirf ek katora pani rakha tha. Uska dil bhar aaya.

Ali ne apni Ammi se kaha, "Ammi, Zohra Bi ke paas Iftari ke liye kuch nahi hai." Safia ne muskura kar kaha, "Beta, Ramzan ka asal maqsad hi dusron ka dard baantna hai".

Ali ne badi khushi se khajur, phal aur doodh ek tokri mein rakha. Safia ne garam salan aur tazi rotiyan bhi saath mein di.

Ali woh bhari hui tokri lekar Zohra Bi ke ghar pahuncha. "Zohra Bi, yeh aapke liye hai." Unhone khushi se Ali ka chehra chooma aur dheron duayein di.

Azaan hui aur Ali ne mehsoos kiya ke apna pet bharne se kahin zyada sukoon dusron ki bhook mitane mein milta hai.

"Khushiyon ka Chiragh hamesha dusron ki madad se jalta hai."

Khushiyon ka Chiragh

By Sana Rahman

Ek chota sa ladka tha jiska naam Idrish tha. Idrish bahut hi mehnti aur samajhdaar tha. Uska sapna tha ki woh apne gaon ke har ghar mein khushiyon ka chiragh jalaye.

Idrish ne dekha ki gaon ke log andhere se pareshan hain. Usne himmat nahi haari aur apni thodi si jamapoonji se mitti ke chiragh aur tel kharida. Woh roz shaam ko gaon ki galiyon mein nikal jata aur jahan andhera hota, wahan ek chiragh jala deta.

Dheere-dheere gaon ke log Idrish ki is nek niyati se prabhavit hue. Unhone bhi Idrish ka saath dena shuru kiya. Idrish ne sikhaya ki agar hum sab mil kar thodi si mehnat karein, toh andhera hamesha ke liye khatam ho sakta hai.

Aaj Idrish ka gaon roshni se jagmaga raha hai, aur log use "Khushiyon ka Chiragh" dene wala farishta maante hain.

"Sana ka World Bank: Mehnat aur imandari hi asli kamyabi hai."
