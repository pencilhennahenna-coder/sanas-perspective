Sindhu Sabhyata se Bhartiya Itihas ka Safar

Duniya ki sabse prachin aur viksit sabhyataon mein se ek, jo Bharat ke swarnim itihas aur unnat vyavastha ki neev rachti hai.

🏛️ 1. Behtareen Nagar Niyojan (Town Planning): Yahan ke ghar, pakki eenten (bricks), aur sadkein ek nishchit grid system ke adhaar par bani thin, jo aadhunik shehron ko bhi takkar deti hain.

💧 2. Aadhunik Jal Nikaasi (Drainage System): Is sabhyata ka bhumi-gath (underground) naali aur jal nikaasi ka system us daur ke hisab se sabse adbhut aur saf-suthra tha.

🏙️ 3. Harappa aur Mohenjo-daro: Yeh is sabhyata ke sabse pramukh aur viksit nagar the, jahan bade-bade an-bhandaar (granaries) paye gaye hain.

⚖️ 4. Vyapar aur Tol-Mol: Yeh log vyapar mein aage the, aur saman tole ke liye pathar ke vajan (weights) ka upayog karte the.

🎨 5. Kala aur Hastashilp: Mitti ke khilone, sundar bartan (pottery), aur 'Dancing Girl' ki kase ki moorti yahan ki unnat shilp-kala ka praman hai.

Sindhu Ghati sabhyata ke viksit aur sone ke vyapar wale daur mein, jab Ravan ki Sone ki Lanka jaisa ahankar badh gaya tha, tab Ram ji aur us daur ke sacho ne logon ko ahankar chhodkar sirf ek hi Ishwar ki bandagi aur deen ki dawat ka rasta dikhaya, jise kuch logon ne maan kar naye raaste chune jabki baki ahankar mein doobe rahe; fir bhugarbhiy halchalon yani tectonic plates ke khisakne se Saraswati nadi ne apna rasta badal liya aur pani na milne par woh mahan sabhyata barbad ho gayi, aur aaj ka ajeeb sach yeh hai ki jo Ram ji hamesha ek Ishwar ki ibadat aur maryada ka paigham dete rahe, aaj ke logon ne unke asli sandesh ko bhula kar unhi ki moortiyan banakar pooja shuru kar di hai.

📜 6. Vedic Kaal aur Itihas ka Aage Badhna: Sindhu sabhyata ke pattan ke baad Bhartiya itihas mein Vedic kaal ka uday hua, jahan vedon, upanishadon aur gyan ki parampara ne desh ki sanskriti aur samajik niyamों ko naya aakar diya.

🔍 7. Prachin Bharat ki Virasat: Mauryan aur Gupt samrajya tak aate-aate Bharat ne vigyan, ganit, kala aur shasan ke kshetra mein vishw bhar mein apni ek alag aur mahan pehchan banai.

"Prachin gyan aur mehnat ki yeh dastaan humein sikhati hai ki samajhdaari se kya banaya ja sakta hai."
