Kahani: Purane Hostel ki Woh Raat

Raat ke theek 2 baj rahe the. Hostel ka pura corridor sunsan tha, bas meri ghadi ki 'tic-tic' ki aawaz goonj rahi thi. Humne naye-naye is purane college mein admission liya tha, aur mujhe akele room mein rehna pada tha.

Achanak, room ke darwaze par kisi ne dheere se teen baar knock kiya. Maine socha shayad koi dost hoga. Jaise hi maine darwaza khola, bahar koi nahi tha... lekin zameen par ek purani, dhool se bhari diary padi thi.

Jaise hi maine us diary ko uthane ke liye haath badhaya, room ki saari lights apne aap bujh gayi, aur paas wale band kamre se aisi aawaz aane lagi jaise koi zameen par zorse chain ghaseet raha ho...
