🇮🇳 Kanoon aur Hamare Adhikar

Apne kanooni adhikaaron (Rights) ko jaanna har Hindustani ke liye zaroori hai.

Buniyadi Adhikar (Fundamental Rights):

  • Samanta ka Adhikar: Kanoon ki nazar mein sab barabar hain.
  • Shiksha ka Adhikar (RTE): 6 se 14 saal tak ke har bache ko muft aur zaroori shiksha ka adhikar hai.
  • Bolne ki Azadi: Apni baat rakhne aur sawal puchne ka adhikar hamara samvidhan deta hai.
  • Sushasan (Good Governance): Sarkari schemes aur kanoon ki jankari hona tarakki ki chabi hai.

Buniyadi Adhikar (Fundamental Rights):

  • Aam Kanoon aur Suraksha (Everyday Laws & Safety) ​Rozmarra ki zindagi mein surakshit rehne aur apne bachaav ke liye har nagrik ko in buniyaadi kanooni baaton ki jankari honi chahiye: ​Cyber Crime aur Online Fraud: Internet ya online len-den ke dauran agar koi thagi ho jaye, toh ghabraye nahi. Turant national cyber helpline number 1930 par call karein ya cybercrime.gov.in par apni shikayat darj karayein. ​Consumer Rights (Grahak Adhikar): Saman kharidte waqt hamesha pakka bill (GST Invoice) lene ki maang karein. Agar dukandar kharab saman deta hai ya bill nahi deta, toh aap grahak forum mein iski shikayat kar sakte hain. ​Mahila Suraksha aur Emergency Numbers: Kisi bhi sankat ya emergency ki sthiti mein turant sahayta ke liye All-in-One emergency number 112 ya mahila helpline number 1091 ka upyog karein. ​FIR Darj Karane ka Adhikar: Agar koi apradh hota hai, toh har nagrik ko yeh adhikar hai ki woh najdiki police station mein jakar apni FIR (First Information Report) darj karaye. Police bina kisi vajah ke FIR likhne se mana nahi kar sakti. ​"Kanoon ki sahi jankari hi humari sabse badi suraksha hai."
