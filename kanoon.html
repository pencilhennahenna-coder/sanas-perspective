🇮🇳 Kanoon aur Hamare Adhikar

Apne kanooni adhikaaron (Rights) ko jaanna har Hindustani ke liye zaroori hai.

Buniyadi Adhikar (Fundamental Rights):

  • Samanta ka Adhikar: Kanoon ki nazar mein sab barabar hain.
  • Shiksha ka Adhikar (RTE): 6 se 14 saal tak ke har bache ko muft aur zaroori shiksha ka adhikar hai.
  • Bolne ki Azadi: Apni baat rakhne aur sawal puchne ka adhikar hamara samvidhan deta hai.
  • Sushasan (Good Governance): Sarkari schemes aur kanoon ki jankari hona tarakki ki chabi hai.

"Kanoon ki jankari hi asli suraksha hai."
