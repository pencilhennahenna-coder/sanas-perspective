Hazrat Ali Ibn Abi Talib (R.A.)

Aap Allah ke Sher (Asadullah) hain aur ilm ka darwaza hain. Bachon mein sabse pehle Islam lane wale aur Rasool-Allah (S.A.W.) ke chachazad bhai aur damad hain. Aapki bahaduri aur hikmat (wisdom) poori ummat ke liye misal hai.
