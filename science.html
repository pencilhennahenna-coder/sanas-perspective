Quran aur Science

Quran Pak mein Allah Ta'ala ne kaaynat ke aise raaz bataye hain jinhe science aaj dhoond rahi hai.

  • Big Bang Theory: Quran mein zikr hai ki ye kaaynat pehle ek thi, phir ise alag kiya gaya.
  • Pani se Zindagi: Science kehti hai har zinda cheez pani se bani hai, aur Quran ne ye 1400 saal pehle farmaya.
  • Suraj ka Rasta: Science ne ab jana ki suraj apne madaar (orbit) mein ghoom raha hai, jo Quran ne pehle hi bata diya tha.

"Wahi hai jisne raat aur din banaye, aur suraj aur chand bhi. Har ek apne raste par ghoom raha hai." (Surah Al-Anbiya)
