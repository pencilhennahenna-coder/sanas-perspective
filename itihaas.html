Islam ka Azeem Itihaas

Hamara itihaas mahan yoddhao aur mahan ulemaon ki dastaano se bhara hua hai.

1. Hazrat Umar Farooq (R.A): Aapka daur-e-hukumat insaf aur tarakki ke liye jana jata hai.

2. Baitul Hikmah (House of Wisdom): Baghdad ka wo mashhoor markaz jahan duniya bhar ka ilm ikatha kiya gaya tha.

3. Salahuddin Ayyubi: Wo mahan Fateh jinhone apni bahaduri aur raham-dili se dushmano ka dil bhi jeet liya tha.

Itihaas humein sikhata hai ki kaise ilm aur imaan ke zor par duniya ko badla ja sakta hai.
